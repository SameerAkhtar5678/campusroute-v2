What the app does

  1. Tap, don't scroll. No dropdowns — tap where you are, tap where you're going, and the shortest route appears instantly. Reverse it, add stops, watch Dijkstra think step by step.
  2. Design your campus. Place gates, hostels, parking — or photograph any place and upload the picture to make a new type of marker. Drag from one place to another to draw roads.
  3. Junctions make themselves. Wherever two roads cross, a junction node is created — and it stays exactly on the crossing as you drag things around. Road lengths re-measure automatically (9 px = 1 m).
  4. Live updates. Anyone can post what's happening — "tree fell, road blocked" — with a photo. Clear reports close or reopen roads automatically; vague ones go to the admin.
  5. Control room. The admin sees a situation brief, decides each case, tunes the automatic rules and adds signal words. Everything is logged.
